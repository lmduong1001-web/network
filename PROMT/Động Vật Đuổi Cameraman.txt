TIMELINE – 15s (CHASE → IMPACT → EMOTION)
0–2s (HOOK)

Camera already recording.
Man looks uneasy, scanning slightly.

Dialogue: “…this feels off…”

2–4s (UNEASE)

Wind moves grass and leaves.
Subtle background movement.
Man doesn’t notice.

Dialogue: “…too quiet…”

4–6s (REVEAL)

Tiger slowly emerges from grass.

Dialogue: “OH—! RUN—!”

Tiger rapidly closes distance.

Camera drops with the body (important: same camera, falls naturally).

No cut, no angle change — just natural fall motion.

Dialogue: “AH—!”

Man on the ground, breathing heavily.
Camera tilted sideways but still recording.

Tiger stands over or beside him.
No attack behavior — just dominance presence.

Dialogue: “…don’t… don’t…”

Tiger lowers its head.
Slowly and gently licks the man’s face or hand (clear contact).

Man freezes → breathing slows → disbelief.
He slowly raises a hand (slight trembling) and touches the tiger.

Tiger stays calm, close, relaxed.

Dialogue: “…easy… easy…”
